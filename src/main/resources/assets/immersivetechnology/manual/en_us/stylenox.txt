StyleNOX Reactor
Going critical!
<&multiblock>The StyleNOX is a Magnox type gas cooled reactor. Loaded with eight §lMagnox Fuel Elements§r and fed §lDistilled Water§r and §lCarbon Dioxide§r, it boils water into §lSteam§r for up to four Steam Turbines.
Starting the reactor draws Flux over <config;i;stylenox.startup_time> ticks from its internal buffer of <config;i;stylenox.energy_capacity> Flux. Once critical it sustains itself; the control rod setting (25% to 100%) decides how many steam channels are live and how fast the fuel burns up. The SCRAM button drops the rods and shuts the reactor down instantly.
Water enters through the basement ring, steam leaves at the four upper gallery corners, carbon dioxide and power connect at the side ducts, and fuel is loaded through the rear duct. Spent elements are pushed into an inventory behind the fuel port.
