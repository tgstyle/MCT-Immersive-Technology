Gas Turbine
GAS GAS GAS!
<&multiblock> The Gas Turbine uses a fuel and an ignition source from the combustors inside the turbine to make hot, high velocity gas. The hot, high velocity gas turns the turbine blades to create rotational energy.

§lOperation§r
    - Liquid fuel is inputted through the back.
    - On the side with the MV energy input is the spark plug which needs to be powered at all times.
    - On the side with the HV energy input is the starter which needs power until the spark plug ignites the fuel. Once ignited, you must remove power from the starter.
    - The very front of the gas turbine is where you connect a multiblock that uses mechanical power to do work.
