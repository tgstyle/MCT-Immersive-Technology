Gas Turbine
GAS GAS GAS!
<&multiblock>The Gas Turbine uses a fuel and an ignition source from the combustors inside the turbine to make hot, high-velocity gas. The hot, high-velocity gas turns the turbine blades to create rotational energy.


§lLiquid Fuel§r is input by the pipe. §lFlue Gas§r is output under the rotor. The side with MV energy input is the spark plug, which can be powered at all times. The side with HV energy input is the starter, which needs power until the spark plug ignites the fuel. Once ignited, you must remove power from the starter. You need to attach a multiblock to the rotor that uses mechanical power for the turbine to work. The flue gas can be fed to a <link;heat_exchanger;§n§oHeatExchanger§r> to recover more energy. A comparator against any exterior block outputs a signal proportional to current rotor speed.
