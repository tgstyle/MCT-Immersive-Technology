Gas Turbine
GAS GAS GAS!
<&multiblock>The Gas Turbine uses a fuel and an ignition source from the combustors inside the turbine to make hot, high-velocity gas. The hot, high-velocity gas turns the turbine blades to create rotational energy.


§lOperation§r
    - §lLiquid Fuel§r is input by the pipe.
    - §lFlue Gas§r is output under the rotor.
    - The side with MV energy input is the spark plug, which can be powered at all times.
    - The side with HV energy input is the starter, which needs power until the spark plug ignites the fuel. Once ignited, you must remove power from the starter.
    - You need to attach a multiblock to the rotor that uses mechanical power for the turbine to work.
    - A comparator against any exterior block outputs a signal proportional to current rotor speed.
