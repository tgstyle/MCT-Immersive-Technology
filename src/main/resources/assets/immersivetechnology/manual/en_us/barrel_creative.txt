Creative Fluid Barrel
Infinite Supply
A creative/testing-only block that supplies an unlimited amount of a chosen fluid. It does not accept fluid input.

§lOperation§r
    - Right-click with a bucket or container of a fluid to set that as the output fluid.
    - Sneak-right-click with an empty hand to clear the selected fluid.
    - Once set, it actively pushes fluid into all 6 neighboring blocks every tick, up to <config;i;barrel_creative.output_amount> mB/t — effectively unlimited at default settings.
