Melting Crucible
Melting!
<&multiblock>The Melting Crucible uses electricity to melt materials into their molten fluid form. Its cooldown rate when idle depends on the biome's ambient temperature — it cools slower in hot biomes.


§lOperation§r
    - §lFluids§r are input on the back.
    - §lMolten Fluids§r are output on the bottom front.
    - Energy is input on the side. Internal buffer holds <config;i;melting_crucible.energy_capacity> Flux.
    - A comparator against the front control panel outputs a signal proportional to current heat level.
