Alternator
Big and Powerful!
<&multiblock>The Alternator converts mechanical energy into Flux, and can power far more than a single Diesel Generator.



§lOperation§r
    - Needs rotational input from a mechanical energy source — a §lSteam Turbine§r or §lGas Turbine§r — connected at the rear.
    - Output is on the front-left and front-right; produces up to <config;i;alternator.alternator_max_output> Flux/t depending on rotor speed.
    - Internal buffer holds <config;i;alternator.alternator_energy_capacity> Flux. A comparator against any exterior block outputs a signal proportional to the current energy level.
