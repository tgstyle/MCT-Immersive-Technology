Heat Exchanger
Is it hot or is it cool?
<&multiblock>The Heat Exchanger is a countercurrent exchanger — hot fluid enters one side and exits the other as it cools, while the fluid being heated flows the opposite direction through the second loop.

The §lHeat Exchanger§r needs hot fluids to make other fluids. The heat exchanger processes hot, energy-bearing fluids with colder fluids like §lWater§r or §lDistilled Water§r to heat them up into more usable forms. A comparator against any exterior block outputs a signal proportional to how full its internal Flux buffer (<config;i;heat_exchanger.energy_capacity> Flux) is.
