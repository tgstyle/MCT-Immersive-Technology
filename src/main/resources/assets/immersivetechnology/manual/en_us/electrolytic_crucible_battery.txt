Electrolytic Crucible Battery
Advanced Electrolysis
<&multiblock>The Electrolytic Crucible Battery performs electrolysis on molten salts and other fluids, producing multiple outputs at once. It can run up to 3 batches in parallel.

§lFluid§r and §lEnergy§r are supplied from the side. Internal buffer holds <config;i;electrolytic_crucible_battery.energy_capacity> Flux. The two separated fluids are output from the top ports at the back; items and spent electrolyte from the bottom. Up to 3 batches process in parallel, so throughput can exceed what one batch's time alone would suggest. A comparator against any exterior block outputs a signal proportional to how full the first fluid output tank is.
