Distiller
Evaporation!
<&multiblock>The Distiller evaporates water into §lDistilled Water§r with salt as a byproduct.




§lOperation§r
    - §lWater§r is input on the left.
    - §lDistilled Water§r is output on the right.
    - Salt is output in the back.
    - A comparator against the front control panel outputs a signal proportional to how full its internal Flux buffer (<config;i;distiller.energy_capacity> Flux) is.
