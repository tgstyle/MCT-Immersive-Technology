Boiler Tank
Getting steamy in here!
<&multiblock>The Boiler Tank is the core of the Boiler system — it holds water and turns it into steam, but it can't produce heat on its own. Attach one §lBoiler (Liquid Fuel)§r or §lBoiler (Solid Fuel)§r burner to supply it.




§lOperation§r
    - §lWater§r is input near the tank on the left side; §lSteam§r is output from the top.
    - Holds up to <config;i;boiler_tank.tank_capacity> mB of water.
    - Runs a batch once the attached burner's heat reaches working temperature.
    - If heat drops mid-batch, progress drains back gradually rather than resetting outright — a brief interruption costs some progress, not all of it.
    - A comparator against any exterior block outputs a signal proportional to current heat level.
