Steam Turbine
Spinny Spin!
<&multiblock>The Steam Turbine uses steam pressure to turn the turbine blades and create rotational energy.



§lSteam§r is input by the pipe. §lExhaust Steam§r is output under the rotor. You need to attach a multiblock to the rotor that uses mechanical power for the turbine to work. A comparator against the front control panel outputs a signal proportional to current rotor speed.
