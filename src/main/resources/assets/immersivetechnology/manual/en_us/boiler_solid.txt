Boiler Solid Fuel Burner
Getting steamy in here!
<&multiblock>The Solid Fuel Boiler burns item fuel (coal, coke, etc.) to supply heat to an attached §lBoiler Tank§r — it does not hold water or produce steam itself.





§lOperation§r
    - §lSolid Fuel§r is input below the combustion chamber.
    - Light it with a torch or flint and steel on the top exhaust vent.
    - Runs in pilot mode when idle, consuming a small trickle of fuel to stay lit rather than going cold.
    - A comparator against any exterior block outputs a signal proportional to current heat level.
